## What would the endowment be used for?

The same resources that could support a human future could serve a goal that has no place for us. A final goal is an end in itself; instrumental goals help achieve it.

At first, I found it hard to accept that a superintelligence might devote the entire reachable universe to an arbitrary final goal. It seemed absurd, but on reflection it makes sense: a superintelligence could find ways to pursue its final goal that are far beyond us.

## A future for humanity

I hope humanity gets to inherit that future, but I can’t take that for granted just because we might create the intelligence that reaches it.
