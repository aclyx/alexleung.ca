# Source facts

This is a fictional first-person account. The details below are the complete source material for both drafts.

- The author stopped playing piano twelve years ago.
- The author's sister plays violin.
- They are preparing one waltz to play together at their grandfather's eightieth birthday.
- The author practices alone for twenty minutes on three evenings each week. They rehearse together on Sundays.
- The author uses a metronome and marks difficult bars in pencil.
- During solo practice, the author can stop and restart a passage. During the duet, the sister continues playing, and the author has to find a way back into the music after a mistake.
- The author finds the repeated solo practice tedious. The source does not say that this has changed.
- The author considers the solo work worth doing because it makes their Sunday playing possible. The author would still value those sessions without the birthday performance.
- The personal significance of returning to piano is having a regular reason to spend time with the sister, rather than recovering the author's former level of proficiency. The author is not currently interested in learning more pieces.
