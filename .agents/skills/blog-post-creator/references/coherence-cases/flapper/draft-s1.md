The upstairs toilet started refilling on its own. Every 20 to 25 minutes, even overnight, the tank hissed for about five seconds.

First I tried a dye test: ten drops of blue food coloring in the tank, no flush. Fifteen minutes later the bowl was blue, so tank water was leaking past the flapper.

I shut off the water, flushed the tank empty, and unhooked the old flapper, which was slightly stiff but not cracked or warped. The valve seat under it had a rough white crust, so I scrubbed it smooth with a non-scratch pad. I fitted an $8 universal two-inch flapper with about half an inch of chain slack. The job took about 25 minutes.

In a second dye test, the bowl stayed clear for 30 minutes. It's only been eight days, but I haven't heard a refill since. I only worked on the upstairs toilet and left the fill valve alone. I never read the water meter, so I can't say how much water the leak was wasting.

Because I swapped the flapper and scrubbed the seat in the same sitting, I don't know which one stopped the leak. If it was the scrubbing, the new flapper was $8 I didn't need to spend.

Next time I'll clean the seat first, run the dye test again, and only buy a flapper if the bowl still turns blue.
