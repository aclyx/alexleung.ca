During one week, they walked each route twice. Each walk on the road route took 18 minutes, bringing them home at 7:48. Each walk on the park route took 27 minutes, bringing them home at 7:57. These four walks are the entire observation period. No delay occurred and no call was missed. There is no evidence about how often delays might occur or how large they might be.

They had expected to prefer the shorter route and were surprised that the extra walking time felt worthwhile. They still value both the quiet and a reliable return. They have not settled on a usual route and are willing to consider changing their departure time. Neither route has a known safety or accessibility problem in this brief.
