## What would the endowment be used for?

A superintelligence’s final goal is what it pursues for its own sake. Acquiring cosmic resources could be an instrumental goal: a means of achieving that end.

At first, the idea of devoting the reachable universe to an arbitrary goal seemed absurd to me. On reflection, more matter and energy could give a superintelligence more computing power to pursue that goal.

## A future for humanity

I hope humanity inherits the cosmic endowment and uses it to support lives beyond Earth. Creating the intelligence that reaches those resources would not guarantee that humanity benefits.
