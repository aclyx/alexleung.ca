# A Basket for the Next Trip Out

Library books waiting to go back tend to land on my kitchen table. Prepaid parcel returns sit on the hallway shelf. I’m considering giving both a single place: an unused basket I already own, on that shelf beside the front door.

The appeal is having one collection point to check instead of two. If I put items there when they are ready to leave, the books would be off the table and I might have less to remember on the way out. A basket beside the door could be a useful visual reminder, assuming I keep noticing it.

I usually run errands on Saturday mornings. My plan would be to check the basket on Friday night and move the relevant items into a carrying bag. That could shift some remembering and packing away from departure. My calendar would still handle deadlines; a basket cannot tell me when a book is due.

I haven’t tried this. I’m assuming the ordinary books and small parcels will fit; oversized parcels would need another place. I also don’t know whether the Friday check would stick. If I try it, the useful question is whether leaving feels easier without creating another pile by the door.
