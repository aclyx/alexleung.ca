# A Basket for the Next Trip Out

Library books waiting to go back tend to land on my kitchen table. Prepaid parcel returns sit on the hallway shelf. My proposal is to give both a single place: an unused basket I already own, on that shelf beside the front door.

One collection point removes the search across two surfaces. Putting each item there when it is ready to leave will keep the kitchen table clear and reduce what I have to remember on the way out. The basket’s position makes it a reliable visual reminder, so ready returns will leave with me instead of getting overlooked.

I usually run errands on Saturday mornings. Checking the basket on Friday night and moving the relevant items into a carrying bag will make those departures easier. Packing ahead takes that small decision out of the moment when I am leaving. My calendar will still handle deadlines; a basket cannot tell me when a book is due.

The ordinary books and small parcels I have in mind should fit. Oversized parcels will need another place. The remaining question is whether I keep up the Friday check. With that habit in place, the arrangement will reduce mental effort without creating another pile by the door.
