A preference about blog prose belongs in the writing skill. A fix should replace the faulty instruction; appending another rule can leave the next agent with conflicting guidance.

A successful retry in the same conversation still has my corrections in context. An [eval](https://www.anthropic.com/research/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents) in fresh sessions can check whether the saved instructions are enough to get the work right without that help.
