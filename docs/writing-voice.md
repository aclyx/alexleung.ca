# Writing Voice

This is the canonical description of Alex's published writing on alexleung.ca. It describes the destination, not a template: the subject should determine the shape of a piece.

## Core Voice

Alex writes compact notes in a calm, direct, technically literate voice. The prose is understated and quietly confident. First person is natural when experience, judgment, or learning matters, but it is not forced into every explanation. Warmth comes from attention to real details rather than decorative personality.

Confidence comes from naming exact objects, mechanisms, constraints, trade-offs, and observed results—not from credentials, promotion, or self-assessment. Supported claims are stated plainly, while uncertainty stays precise. Significance comes through consequences rather than declarations of importance. Titles and headings are usually literal and subject-first, with room for occasional dry wit. Openings bring the artifact, question, event, or scene into view early. A concrete detail can carry its own meaning without a recap telling the reader how to interpret it.

The writing is concise but not breathless. Headings and lists appear when they improve scanning, not as a default shape. Contrast is a recurring reasoning device—one explanation against a more precise one, or a benefit against its cost—but it should mark a real distinction rather than become a cadence template.

## Technical Writing

Technical pieces read expert-to-peer. They explain the useful mechanism and make limits explicit. Exact tools, operations, equations, code, and implementation details appear when they help the reader inspect the reasoning. Claims stay bounded by what was built, tested, read, or observed.

These pieces often end on a decision, practical limit, changed understanding, or unresolved question rather than a broad lesson.

## Personal Writing

Personal pieces begin close to lived experience: a place, object, action, routine, or practical constraint. Ordinary details create the warmth. Chronology can provide enough structure, and interpretation arrives sparingly after the reader has something concrete to see.

The tone remains plain and observant, with occasional lightness rather than polished sentiment. Endings tend to land on a present state, preference, hope, or memorable observation. A trip, move, or purchase does not need to become a general principle.

## Scope

This document describes how the finished writing should sound.
