Codex has become part of how I maintain this site, often from my phone: fixing a layout bug, tightening a sentence, or adjusting spacing by a few pixels. I usually know what I want to change before I start, and I can judge the result on the page. Writing each edit myself matters less when I can still shape what the site says and how it presents it.

My Sora experiments were more exploratory. I could try cover ideas and visual styles while still figuring out what I wanted to make. Choosing what to keep, revise, or discard helped me develop the idea. Most of those experiments stayed as sketches.

In both cases, I can express myself through choices about the result without carrying out every step of its production. That makes working directly in the medium feel less central to me. I still have to work out what I mean and recognize when the result expresses it; delegating the execution gives me another way to put that intention and taste into the work.
