Codex has become part of how I maintain this site, often from my phone: fixing a layout bug, tightening a sentence, or adjusting spacing by a few pixels. I usually know what I want to change before I start, and I can judge the result on the page.

My Sora experiments were more exploratory. I could try cover ideas and visual styles while still figuring out what I wanted to make. Choosing what to keep, revise, or discard helped me develop the idea. Most of those experiments stayed as sketches.

In both cases, my part is working out what I mean and recognizing when the result says it: which spacing looks right on the page, which sketch is worth another pass. Working directly in the medium matters less to me than making those choices, and delegating the execution gives me another way to put my intention and taste into the work.
