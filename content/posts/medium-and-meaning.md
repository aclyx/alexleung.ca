Codex has become part of how I maintain this site, often from my phone: fixing a layout bug, tightening a sentence, or adjusting spacing by a few pixels. I can make one precise change without waiting for a full work session at a desk.

I use Sora earlier in the process, while I am still discovering the idea. I might test a cover direction or visual treatment before I know whether it belongs anywhere. Most of what I make there remains a sketch rather than something I publish.
