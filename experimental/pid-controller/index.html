Model & measurement notes›

u = Kp·error + Ki·∫error dt − Kd·dy/dt. The derivative acts on measured output with a 0.1 s filter; anti-windup limits integral accumulation when the actuator saturates.

Idealized models: no noise, delay, or tank overflow. The controller updates every 0.01 s; RK4 advances the plant between updates. Extreme gains can make fixed-step results unreliable. Drawings scale to each run. These presets are not tuning advice for real equipment.

Settling requires staying within the 2% band through 20 s, for at least 1 s. Final error is rounded to three decimal places.
