Network editor & model

Balanced, steady-state AC power flow using Newton–Raphson on a 100 MVA base. Loads are constant P/Q. Lines use a π model with charging at both ends; transformers support taps and phase shifts. Slack fixes voltage and angle; PV fixes active generation and voltage. Generator reactive-power limits and thermal limits are not enforced. Convergence is not a claim of safe operation.
