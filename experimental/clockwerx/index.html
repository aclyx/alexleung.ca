How to play
  • Swing

    Hold to grab the next peg and change rotation.

  • Flip

    Hold to grab the next peg and keep your rotation.

  • Bounce

    Hold to turn back when the tip meets a peg.

  • Reverse

    Tap to turn back immediately around your current peg.

Use X or period to swing, Shift to flip, Z or slash to bounce, and Space to reverse. Press P to pause.
