a:T178d,<p>OpenAI's mid-year break was last week. We took the opportunity to visit southern Utah. We flew from SFO to Las Vegas, picked up an SUV, and drove toward Zion.</p>
<p>We packed light for the trip. That helped because we were moving around most days: a stay near Zion, a few days at Ofland near Escalante, and a lot of driving between hikes.</p>
<h2>Zion and Red Canyon</h2>
<p>The first full day was very full: Watchman's Trail in Zion, Pink Ridge in Red Canyon, Mossy Cave, and then the drive to Escalante.</p>
<p>Watchman's Trail was a good first hike because Zion immediately feels tall. The photo is basically what I remember: the top of the cliff in sun, the lower canyon still in shadow.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/zion-watchman-content.webp" alt="Zion cliffs seen from Watchman&#x27;s Trail in morning light" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/zion-watchman-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/zion-watchman-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="1366"></p>
<p>Pink Ridge was smaller and more concentrated. The formations were closer to the trail, with pine trees breaking up the orange rock.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/red-canyon-pink-ridge-content.webp" alt="Orange hoodoos and pine trees along the Pink Ridge trail in Red Canyon" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/red-canyon-pink-ridge-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/red-canyon-pink-ridge-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="1366"></p>
<p>Mossy Cave was more of a visit than a full hike. We stopped by on the way.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/mossy-cave-content.webp" alt="Mossy Cave with a trail sign and overhanging orange rock" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/mossy-cave-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/mossy-cave-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="1366"></p>
<h2>Ofland</h2>
<p>After that we headed toward Escalante. Scenic Byway 12 was beautiful, and because we had an SUV, the drive itself was pretty easy.</p>
<p>At Ofland, we stayed in an Airstream for a few days. It was super cute and more memorable than a normal hotel room.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/ofland-airstream-content.webp" alt="Airstream trailer at Ofland under a bright afternoon sky" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/ofland-airstream-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/ofland-airstream-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="768"></p>
<p>From Ofland, we hiked Lower Calf Creek.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/lower-calf-creek-content.webp" alt="Green creek corridor between pale and red canyon walls at Lower Calf Creek" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/lower-calf-creek-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/lower-calf-creek-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="1366"></p>
<p>Lower Calf Creek felt like a break from the first day. There was more green, more water, and more time walking along the canyon floor between pale and red walls.</p>
<h2>Bryce Canyon</h2>
<p>The next day we did the Figure 8 loop in Bryce Canyon. Bryce was easily my favorite part of the trip.</p>
<p>Before we even dropped into the loop, the overlook had rows of hoodoos, the rim behind them, and more layers fading out in the distance.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-amphitheater-overlook-content.webp" alt="Bryce Canyon amphitheater viewed from above with hoodoos stretching into the distance" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-amphitheater-overlook-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-amphitheater-overlook-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="768"></p>
<p>Once we were down in it, the scale was easier to feel. The columns were big enough that I felt small next to them, but close enough that I could see the lines, crumbling edges, and openings in the rock.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-hoodoo-wall-content.webp" alt="Bryce Canyon hoodoo wall with natural openings under a blue sky" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-hoodoo-wall-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-hoodoo-wall-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="512"></p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-hoodoos-clouds-content.webp" alt="Bryce Canyon hoodoos below a blue sky with white clouds" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-hoodoos-clouds-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-hoodoos-clouds-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="1366"></p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-hoodoo-columns-content.webp" alt="Close view of Bryce Canyon hoodoo columns with clouds behind them" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-hoodoo-columns-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-hoodoo-columns-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="1366"></p>
<p>What I liked most about Bryce was how obvious the geologic history was. You could see the layers and erosion everywhere.
