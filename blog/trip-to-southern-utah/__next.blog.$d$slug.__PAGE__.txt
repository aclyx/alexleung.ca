a:T1589,<p>We flew from SFO to Las Vegas, picked up an SUV, and drove toward Zion. We packed light because we were moving around most days: a stay near Zion, a few days at Ofland near Escalante, and a lot of driving between hikes.</p>
<h2>Zion and Red Canyon</h2>
<p>On the first full day, we hiked Watchman Trail in Zion and Pink Ledges Trail in Red Canyon, stopped at Mossy Cave, and then drove to Escalante.</p>
<p>Watchman Trail was a good first hike because Zion immediately feels tall. The photo is basically what I remember: the top of the cliff in sun, the lower canyon still in shadow.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/zion-watchman-content.webp" alt="Zion cliffs seen from Watchman Trail in morning light" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/zion-watchman-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/zion-watchman-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="1366"></p>
<p>Pink Ledges Trail was smaller, with formations closer to the trail and pine trees breaking up the orange rock.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/red-canyon-pink-ridge-content.webp" alt="Orange hoodoos and pine trees along Pink Ledges Trail in Red Canyon" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/red-canyon-pink-ridge-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/red-canyon-pink-ridge-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="1366"></p>
<p>We stopped at Mossy Cave on the way to Escalante.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/mossy-cave-content.webp" alt="Mossy Cave with a trail sign and overhanging orange rock" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/mossy-cave-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/mossy-cave-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="1366"></p>
<h2>Ofland</h2>
<p>Scenic Byway 12 took us from Mossy Cave toward Escalante.</p>
<p>At Ofland, we stayed in an Airstream for a few days. It was super cute.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/ofland-airstream-content.webp" alt="Airstream trailer at Ofland under a bright afternoon sky" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/ofland-airstream-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/ofland-airstream-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="768"></p>
<p>From Ofland, we hiked Lower Calf Creek.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/lower-calf-creek-content.webp" alt="Green creek corridor between pale and red canyon walls at Lower Calf Creek" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/lower-calf-creek-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/lower-calf-creek-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="1366"></p>
<p>Lower Calf Creek felt like a break from the first day. We walked along the canyon floor with more green and water between pale and red walls.</p>
<h2>Bryce Canyon</h2>
<p>The next day we did the Figure 8 loop in Bryce Canyon.</p>
<p>Before we even dropped into the loop, the overlook had rows of hoodoos, the rim behind them, and more layers fading out in the distance.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-amphitheater-overlook-content.webp" alt="Bryce Canyon amphitheater viewed from above with hoodoos stretching into the distance" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-amphitheater-overlook-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-amphitheater-overlook-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="768"></p>
<p>Once we were down in it, I felt small next to the columns. Up close, I could see the lines, crumbling edges, and openings in the rock.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-hoodoo-wall-content.webp" alt="Bryce Canyon hoodoo wall with natural openings under a blue sky" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-hoodoo-wall-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-hoodoo-wall-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="512"></p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-hoodoos-clouds-content.webp" alt="Bryce Canyon hoodoos below a blue sky with white clouds" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-hoodoos-clouds-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/trip-to-southern-utah/bryce-hoodoos-clouds-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="1366"></p>
