17:T96c,<p>My wife and I spent two nights in Desolation Wilderness over Labor Day weekend. We rented a car and drove there on the Saturday before Labor Day. The drive took four hours, and we started hiking around 4:30.</p>
<p>Granite Lake was two kilometers from the trailhead. We camped there for the first night.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/two-nights-in-desolation-wilderness/granite-lake-camp-content.webp" alt="Our first campsite beside Granite Lake, with backpacks and cooking gear on the granite" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/two-nights-in-desolation-wilderness/granite-lake-camp-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/two-nights-in-desolation-wilderness/granite-lake-camp-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="768"></p>
<p>On the second day, we made our way to Upper Velma Lake, probably seven kilometers.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/two-nights-in-desolation-wilderness/upper-velma-morning-content.webp" alt="A cup held in front of Upper Velma Lake and the granite above it" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/two-nights-in-desolation-wilderness/upper-velma-morning-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/two-nights-in-desolation-wilderness/upper-velma-morning-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="683"></p>
<p>The campsites were not very cold, but it was super windy and loud. We tried a new sleep setup for the trip, the Zenbivy Double Sleeping System. It was great: warm and roomy, but it took up more pack space. We also got a new bear canister for the trip. We had more food than we needed, so we probably could have packed lighter.</p>
<p>On the last day, we hiked all the way out.</p>
<p><img src="/assets/blog/two-nights-in-desolation-wilderness/desolation-granite-trail-content.webp" alt="Pines in front of broad granite ridges in Desolation Wilderness" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low" sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 896px, 100vw" srcset="/assets/blog/two-nights-in-desolation-wilderness/desolation-granite-trail-content-sm.webp 640w, /assets/blog/two-nights-in-desolation-wilderness/desolation-granite-trail-content.webp 1024w" width="1024" height="768"></p>
