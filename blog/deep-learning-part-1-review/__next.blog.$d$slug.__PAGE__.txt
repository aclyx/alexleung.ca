<h2>Shared vocabulary for later chapters</h2>
<p>What I got most from Part I was its quick pass through linear algebra, probability, and numerical computation, which put the background for the later chapters back in view without a long detour. Linear regression in <a href="https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/ml.html">Chapter 5</a>, for example, brings linear algebra and probability together.
